Four names appear around this product, and flattening them into one would be
wrong in at least one place that matters legally. So, precisely:

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Kovati** | The company and the brand. Automation Forge is its product. |
| **MetaWorx LLC** | Founded Kovati. **Holds all the IP and all business transactions.** Copyright follows the IP, so `© MetaWorx LLC`. |
| **Blackcode SA** | Swiss. Backs Kovati, and **runs the cloud infrastructure the account sits on today**. |
| **Colony Origins** | The Unreal Engine production Automation Forge is built and proven inside, before anything ships. |

**Two companies back Kovati: MetaWorx LLC and Blackcode SA.** Not three.

## Where your account data actually sits

This is a legal fact about whose cloud account holds the records, not a
branding choice, and it does not follow the logo.

Today the **data controller is Blackcode SA**, because the infrastructure runs
on Blackcode's account. That is temporary — the infrastructure moves to
Kovati's own, and the controller becomes MetaWorx on the same day.

We are saying so here rather than letting it change quietly, because a privacy
claim that is technically true and practically stale is exactly the kind of
thing this company has decided not to do. The authoritative version is
always [the privacy page](https://kovati.dev/privacy).

## Colony Origins

Automation Forge is built inside a real game production and nothing ships from
it that has not first been used there.

That is the only quality gate we trust. A plugin written against an imagined
project passes every test its author thought of. A plugin used to actually
finish a scene fails in specific, unglamorous ways — the skeleton faces the
wrong axis, the prop leaves the hand mid-montage, the face montage plays on
the body mesh, the NPC cached its bark at spawn and never heard the new one.

Where a page in this wiki tells you something oddly specific, it is because
the general version was wrong once, in a scene someone was trying to finish.
