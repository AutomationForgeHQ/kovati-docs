## 0.1.x

### 0.1.0

*21 September 2026* · [Download (124 KB)](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/download/meshforgegarmenttoolset-v0.1.0/MeshForgeGarmentToolset-0.1.0-UE5.8-Win64.zip) · [Release on GitHub](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/tag/meshforgegarmenttoolset-v0.1.0)

#### Added
- The Garment Studio as agent tools: open it, capture it from named angles to a PNG, turn joints, place the
  garment, sculpt with drags across the last picture, wrap with fit settings and wait for it, save, finish and
  close.
- The **Fit a garment by sight** skill: the loop, what to look for in each picture, and when to hand over to a
  person.

#### Compatibility
- Needs MeshForge 0.3.0 and MeshForgeGarment 0.2.0 or later. The Garment Studio it drives
  arrived in MeshForgeGarment 0.2.0.
