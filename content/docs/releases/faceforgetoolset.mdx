## 0.2.x

### 0.2.2

*11 September 2026*

#### Changed
- `CloneFaceBankForLanguage` records the source face bank on the clone. That is what lets the
  clone's clips follow the source-language performance underneath a dub instead of guessing the
  acting from audio.
- The version this toolset reports to an agent is now read from the plugin's own
  descriptor rather than repeated in C++, so it can no longer answer a number the
  installed package does not carry.

### 0.2.1

*8 September 2026*

- Packaging fix: the release now carries everything the register allows. `BuildPlugin`'s filter excludes `Config/` and every `public_extra` path, so earlier zips shipped without them.
- `GetToolsetVersion()` answers this plugin's real version; it had drifted from the descriptor.

### 0.2.0

*7 September 2026*

- Face clips gain layers; the mix is a recipe, not a rerun
- Merge crosses vocabularies; mouth layers override only what they drive
- The culmination wiring: one library, a teleprompter, and panels that meet
- Casting made human-readable and controllable: audition, discovered actions, a stage
- A re-voicing given its own path, distinct from a new take
- The take became the unit: four quadrants, folders, one decision
- Hear a voice without committing to it, and a face that gets made
- Face bank now links to its speech bank by id instead of guessing
- Speech and face joined by id: a speaker names their head, a bank says what it holds
- Hand correction shipped as a layer, so the solve underneath can be replaced
- Localisation: a sibling bank per language, joined by line id
- A face bank now answers what a line plays, and takes outside buttons
- Repointed to kovati.dev
