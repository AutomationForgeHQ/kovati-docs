## 0.2.x

### 0.2.2

*11 September 2026* · [Download (702 KB)](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/download/faceforge-v0.2.2/FaceForge-0.2.2-UE5.8-Win64.zip) · [Release on GitHub](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/tag/faceforge-v0.2.2)

#### Added
- A cloned face bank now remembers the bank it was cloned from, and solving a clip whose sibling
  there already carries layers **follows that performance** instead of solving from audio alone:
  every layer is copied in order, the audio-driven ones re-solved from this clip's own audio, and
  the result merged. A dubbed line keeps the acting that was captured under the new words.
- A second solve on a following clip reports it already current rather than redoing the work.

#### Changed
- A correction authored against the source clip rides across to the clip that follows it; one
  authored on the localised clip is kept.

### 0.2.0

*7 September 2026* · [Download (690 KB)](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/download/faceforge-v0.2.0/FaceForge-0.2.0-UE5.8-Win64.zip) · [Release on GitHub](https://github.com/AutomationForgeHQ/releases/releases/tag/faceforge-v0.2.0)

- Face clips gain layers; the mix is a recipe, not a rerun
- Merge crosses vocabularies; mouth layers override only what they drive
- Casting made human-readable and controllable: audition, discovered actions, a stage
- The Face Bank stage now frames the face, not the back of the head
- Face Bank editor: montage-editor-style stage, voice-with-face preview, an inspector, transport
  controls and orbit-or-free camera
- The take became the unit: four quadrants, folders, one decision
- Fixed: solving a bank could overwrite a merged face
- The plan for editing and graduating a face written down (`GRADUATION.md`)
- Fixed: preview mesh list not following the bank; face preview drawing at the wrong detail level;
  needed its own preview component rather than a re-pointed one
- Face bank now links to its speech bank by id instead of guessing
- Tools menu consolidated into one Automation Forge section
- Every Forge asset now has a factory; double-click opens the panel that owns it
- Hand correction shipped as a layer, so the solve underneath can be replaced
- Localisation: a sibling bank per language, joined by line id
- A face bank now answers what a line plays, and takes outside buttons
- Repointed to kovati.dev
