## 0.4.x

### 0.4.0

*20 November 2026* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

**The first release.** SkinForge stamps scars, tattoos, wounds, implants, face paint and skin
detail onto a character as layers, previews them live on its own materials, then bakes them into
new copies of its textures and new material instances. The originals are never edited. Every bake
is a named version, and Apply and Revert switch a character, its Blueprints and its placed actors
between versions. A starter library of 60 marks in five packs comes with it.

SkinForge is a paid product with a free Lite tier, in one download.

#### Added
- **Lite and Full.** Without a licence SkinForge runs as Lite, free: every baked map is at most
  2K (2048 on its longer side), a glow map too, and Upscale Base Color is shown greyed and marked
  "Full". With a licence that includes SkinForge it runs as Full: maps are baked at their source
  size, and Upscale Base Color enlarges base colour to 4K or 8K. Nothing else differs, and nothing
  is watermarked. The panel shows the tier under the Bake button, and a Lite bake says it once in
  its notes, which the Bake Skin Set and Stamp Skin Patch tools return.
- **How the tier is decided.** SkinForge reads the signed licence the Kovati Hub keeps on this
  computer. Sign in to the Hub with an account that owns SkinForge and it runs as Full; signed out,
  or without SkinForge in the licence, it runs as Lite. The licence keeps working offline for 30
  days after the Hub last refreshed it. Full is bought at
  [kovati.dev](https://kovati.dev/plugins/skinforge/). `SkinForge.Tier.ForceLite 1` runs a
  licensed copy as Lite, for testing.
- Everything the development history below describes, as its latest section leaves it.

#### Compatibility
- **Unreal Engine 5.8, Windows (Win64), editor only.** Installed through the Kovati Hub.
- **SkinForgeToolset 0.3.1**, the free, open-source agent toolset, pairs with this version.
- Works on skeletal meshes, assembled MetaHumans included: their baked textures and materials.
  Marks are not written into an editable MetaHuman Character, so they are not part of a
  re-assembly in MetaHuman Creator.

#### Known issues
- **An apply to a MetaHuman placed in a level lasts until its construction script runs again**
  (moving the actor, compiling its Blueprint, reloading the level), and it is never saved. The
  apply report says so. To keep a version on a character, apply it to its Blueprint or its assets.
- **A wrapped band round a limb reaches about 80° each side of its centre.** A full ring needs two
  layers, or a lower *Min Facing*.
- **A set's first glowing bake compiles a copy of the skin material in the background.** Until it
  finishes, the editor draws the skin without the glow; a notification stays up until it is done.
- **Experimental.** File formats, settings and saved data may change between versions. Keep
  backups.

## 0.3.x

### 0.3.5

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Glowing marks come in a colour.** LED, UV circuit and the lit barcode now have a Colour choice:
  cyan-teal (the default), magenta or amber. The glow spills a few millimetres onto the skin round
  it, and the part under the skin is darker, so the mark still reads when the light is off. The
  LED cores are at full brightness.
- **`SkinForge::RenderReady(Set, Version)`**, for scripts. It says whether a version's baked
  materials are compiled, its textures built and its glow maps resident, and lists what is still
  pending. Poll it before taking a picture.

#### Changed
- **Scars and wounds remade from clinical photographs** in the starter library:
  - **Cut, claw and surgical scars:** the line changes width along its length, is raised and
    puckered where it has healed, and the surgical scar shows its suture-track dots. The healed
    tissue reads on medium and dark skin, not only pale skin.
  - **Bullet and shrapnel scars** have a visible crater and pucker, with the skin pulled in round
    them and deeper height, so they read at a normal viewing distance.
  - **Graze** is less red and less busy: a wet raw patch, then a scab.
  - **Stab wound** is bigger and stronger.
  - **Bruise** is as strong as the black eye, with soft swelling. On Day 4 its edge turns
    yellow-green round a purple core.
  - **Fresh cigarette burns and burn blisters** have uneven raw centres that look wet and dark, not
    rings that glow.
  - **Staples** are visible steel: grey-silver, shiny and raised, about 6 mm across.
- **Small marks have twice the texture resolution.** Jacks, acne, bullet and shrapnel scars,
  cigarette burns and the small tattoos stay crisp up close.
- **A fresh tattoo is less glossy.** Its own Fresh roughness offset is now -0.05 (was -0.15).
- **Face paint and skin detail:**
  - **The Cracked look of the paints cracks and flakes.** Camo, finger stripes, mud, soot, blood smear and the war band dry into a network of fine cracks with flakes gone. Before, it barely changed, or showed smooth wavy lines.
  - **Camo** is matte olive, brown and black greasepaint with the pores showing through, and thin, uneven edges with no straight side.
  - **Finger stripes** are dragged and streaky, with dry gaps, in a darker red ochre.
  - **Mud** is a lighter grey-tan, so it shows on dark skin.
  - **Sweat** has beads, droplets and run trails that change only the shine and add a little relief. The orange stain on "Dripping" is gone.
  - **Freckles** have a Sun control from a winter dusting to dense, dark summer freckles.
  - **Sunburn** has a Strength control, from a blush to a deep red burn with a slight sheen and thin peeling.
- **Healed scars read on any skin.** The "Healed pale" look of the cut, claw, surgical and shrapnel scars is
  clearly paler than dark skin and a touch paler on fair skin. The healed tissue is smoother and slightly
  glossier, and stands a little proud or sunken. The fresh stages are unchanged.
- **The healing surgical scar** varies in width and has suture tracks across it and a broken-up tone.
- **The black eye on Day 4** keeps its purple in the lid crease and under the lashes, round it a mottled
  yellow-green that breaks up into the skin, with soft, irregular edges.
- **Summer freckles** are denser and darker across the nose and cheekbones.
- **The glowing barcode lights every bar evenly**, in all three colours. Before, thin bars glowed less, so
  part of the code looked dimmer.

#### Fixed
- **A fresh tattoo's broad dark fills no longer glare like chrome.** The ointment film thins out to
  a quarter where the ink is dense, so a black fill reads as wet ink. It stays full on the red halo
  and on the skin between lines.
- **Glow now shows on a MetaHuman in every session.** 0.3.4's glow could vanish for two reasons:
  - **Its maps never streamed in after the editor restarted.** A glow map is named only by a
    material instance's override, so it had no streaming data: the editor kept its smallest mip
    and the glow averaged away. Glow maps are now always resident, about 11 MB for a 4K map.
    Re-bake older glow versions.
  - **The colour washed to pale pink or white.** A MetaHuman's skin is drawn through Unreal's
    subsurface pass, which took the glow with it and lost its colour, so cyan and amber looked the
    same. The glow copy now shades glowing texels as Default Lit, so the glow skips that pass under
    any `r.SSS.Checkerboard` setting, and darkens the skin under the glow by the glow's strength, as
    over an implant. Cyan, magenta and amber keep their colour. A skin with a fixed subsurface
    model divides the glow by its colour instead, which is right with checkerboard SSS on.
  - **The material copy compiled in the background.** A set's first glowing bake makes a copy of
    the skin material, and until its shaders finish the editor draws the skin without the glow.
    The bake still returns at once. Its notes now say which materials glow, from which copy and at
    what intensity, and whether shaders are still compiling. An editor notification stays up until
    they are done.

### 0.3.4

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Glowing marks glow on a MetaHuman.** LED, UV ink and the lit barcode now light up on a baked
  MetaHuman. Before, its skin material had no emissive input, so the glow was dropped.
  - When a set has a glowing layer and the skin has no emissive texture, the bake writes a glow map
    beside the skin's colour map.
  - It also makes a copy of the skin's parent material, with an emissive input added
    ("SkinForge Emissive" and "SkinForge Emissive Intensity").
  - Copies of the material instances between that copy and the slot are made too, with their
    settings kept. All of these copies go in the set's `Glow` folder and are reused by later bakes.
  - The baked instance derives from those copies. The MetaHuman's own materials are never changed.
  - Sets without a glowing layer bake as before.
- **Glow Intensity**, on a skin set (under Bake, default 30, which reads as a lit mark under a low-key
  studio light; daylight scenes need more). It sets how bright that glow is. You can
  change it later on the baked instance.
- **Apply remakes a glowing face's per-actor instances.** A MetaHuman Blueprint makes per-actor
  material instances for its face, and these cannot take the glow as they are. Apply now remakes
  them from the glow version, keeping their own values, and Revert makes them again from what they
  came from.

#### Changed
- **The fresh ointment sheen stays off hardware.** On marks laid over the skin (chrome, titanium,
  plates), a fresh_ring shines only on the skin round the piece. The metal keeps its own roughness.
- **The "Just fitted" presets of the hard-surface marks** (Neural jack, Data jack, Port array,
  Chrome ring) now redden and swell the skin round the implant. Their Irritation control drives
  fresh_ring.

#### Fixed
- **A Fresh session tattoo no longer looks wrapped in cling film.** The 0.3.3 ointment sheen lowered
  roughness by 0.22 over the whole piece, so under an ordinary key light it showed as a white specular
  patch that hid the red halo and made red fills read almost white. It now lowers roughness by 0.07.
  A mark's own Fresh roughness offset still adds on top inside the ink. Albedo is not changed.

### 0.3.3

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Upscale Base Color**, on a skin set (Off, 4K or 8K, under Bake). Base colour maps smaller than
  that size are enlarged before the marks are drawn into them, so fine-line ink stays crisp on a body
  that a Cinematic MetaHuman bakes at 4K. It is off by default. An 8K map takes about 256 MB of memory
  while baking (twice that with Keep Skin Detail), and four times a 4K map's GPU memory and disk.

#### Fixed
- **A Fresh session tattoo now looks freshly done.** Before, it barely differed from a healed one.
  - The inflamed halo is wider (2.5 mm) and stronger, and it is full even round a single fine line.
  - The halo is a shift of the skin's own colour, so it is red on fair skin and a warm flush on dark
    skin, and it never turns black ink maroon.
  - The lines stand slightly proud, and the halo is a little swollen.
  - A glossy ointment film covers the piece and its halo, not only the ink's own coverage.
  - The starter tattoos' presets need no change.
- **Applying over another skin set's version replaces it.** An actor wearing a version from a
  different set holds none of this set's originals, so Apply left that version on. A material that
  SkinForge baked from one of this set's originals is now replaced like this set's own versions,
  and Revert puts the original back. The result names what it replaced.
- **A glowing mark on a material with no emissive input is reported.** A MetaHuman's baked skin
  material has no emissive texture, so a glow cannot be baked into it. The bake now says so, where it
  used to leave the glow out silently. Its colour, relief and shine still bake. To make it glow, give
  the skin material an emissive input, or place a separate emissive mesh over the mark.

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **`ink_blowout`, a new control target:** a faint, blue-shifted haze 1 to 3 mm round a tattoo's line
  work, as when ink spread too deep. It brings back the blown-out look of the v3 tattoos as a
  setting on any ink mark.

#### Changed
- **A smaller, curated starter library of 20 marks.** The best of the earlier marks are kept, with new
  tattoo designs and face paint. Run *Add Starter Decals* once to get the new cards.
  - **Tattoos:**
    - a colour Japanese dragon back piece, 38 x 57 cm at 4K;
    - an American traditional dragon head;
    - fine-line butterflies;
    - a blackwork geometric forearm sleeve;
    - a black-and-grey portrait;
    - the swallow.
  - Every tattoo has ink-colour slots, Healed and Faded stages, *Wear*, *Fresh* and four presets.
  - The first five were designed with OpenAI image generation; Kovati owns the output. They were
    converted to ink slots with crisp line work at full size.
  - **Face paint, a new Paint category:** a war band across the eyes, cheek stripes and an ash
    stripe. They are matte, keep the skin's pores, and have a *Wear* setting that dries them,
    cracks them and flakes them off.
  - **Kept:** the cut, claw, surgical and burn scars, the bruise and the puncture; the neural jack,
    data jack, subdermal LED and glowing barcode.
  - **Removed:** the other starter marks. Marks already placed in a project keep working.

#### Fixed
- **Apply now covers a MetaHuman's body in a level.** On a Cinematic MetaHuman, the Body component
  draws its mesh's own material, with no override. Apply now sets an override with the version's
  material on any component slot that draws one of the set's originals, and Revert puts the
  original back.
- **The per-actor face textures survive that change.** Setting the Body override reruns the
  Blueprint's construction script, which rebuilt the face instances and dropped the textures set a
  moment before. Apply now changes references first and the per-actor instances last.
- **A target's state reads right with several versions.** An instance that only an older version
  replaces no longer makes the target read "Mixed" under a newer one.
- **A skeletal mesh asset is no longer "Unused".** In Unreal 5.8 a skeletal mesh's material list is
  a transient property, so Apply skipped it. It now reads and writes the mesh's slots directly.
- **Big wrapped marks stay on the skin they face.**
  - Wrapped layers now respect *Min Facing* and a depth (the larger of the layer's Depth and half
    the mark's larger side), as planar layers always did.
  - A back piece no longer carries over the shoulders onto the jaw and arms, or round the neck to
    the throat.
  - The wrap keeps only the part of its patch joined to the skin under its centre, so the inside of
    the lips can no longer catch stretched ink from a back piece.
  - Checked with Tattoo_DragonBack at 45 and 55 cm on a Cinematic MetaHuman: the face, throat and
    jaw are clean, and the ink stops at the sides of the neck with a soft edge.
- **A back piece no longer reaches the teeth.** At a level of detail that only the face mesh has, a
  layer's centre is far from every surface, and every slot was then allowed. Now the nearest skin
  is used.

#### Known issues
- A wrapped band round a limb reaches about 80° each side of its centre. A full ring needs two
  layers, or a lower *Min Facing*.

### 0.3.0

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Starter library v4: 43 marks**, by the art agent. Each mark is one card, with its stages,
  inks, controls and presets.
  - **Scars:** Cut, Surgical, Claw, Burn, Bullet, Keloid, Acne and Shrapnel heal along their
    stages. Each has a Pigment choice (pale, pink or dark tissue) and a Depth slider.
  - **Wounds:** Laceration, Stitches, Graze and Puncture have Wet and Depth. A bruise covers day 1,
    day 4 and day 10.
  - **Tattoos:** every tattoo is an ink-slot mark, with its inks in the order Colour, Accent, Shade
    and Line. Each has Healed and Faded stages, Wear and Fresh controls, and presets.
  - **New tattoo designs:** fine-line script, dagger, dotwork, barcode, a rose, a Celtic band, a
    swallow, a clipper ship for the chest, and a 45 cm irezumi dragon back piece (up to 55 cm).
  - **Cyber:** neural and data jacks heal from Irritated to Healed. A skin plate, a glowing barcode
    and a shoulder hardpoint are new, and the subdermal LED glows.
  - **Licences:** every image is our own work, made procedurally, and is listed in `SOURCES.json`.
    One item is flagged for a licence check before sale: lettering rendered from a system font.
- **Changed marks:**
  - The v3 per-stage and per-variant decals are merged into these marks: the cut stages,
    `_Old`, `_Faded` and `_Blowout`.
  - `Scar_Slash_Fresh` becomes Wound_Laceration.
  - The tribal blade band is replaced by the Celtic band, because cultural marks are not copied.
  - Scar_Sunken is dropped.
  - Their old assets keep working where they are used, but they are no longer updated.
- **Marks with stages, inks and controls (format v4).** A mark can hold several looks along the
  layer's *Age*: a cut that goes from fresh to scabbed, healing and old. The bake blends the two
  stages around the Age, image by image. When their blends differ, it lays each one on and
  cross-fades the results.
- **Ink slots.** A tattoo can be drawn once as an ID image, with one part of the design per
  channel. Each layer then picks the ink for each part, so the same design can be black and grey,
  a red and blue traditional piece, or a faded green one.
- **Controls made of data.** A mark names its own sliders, switches and choices, such as Wear,
  Fresh, Pigment or Depth. Each drives a fixed list of things the bake knows: ink depth, density,
  blue-green drift, broken lines, a fresh red ring, the scar's lightness and colour, shine, relief,
  pore smoothing and glow. **Presets** set them all at once.
- **The library has chips.** They are built from the marks' categories, so a new category needs no
  code change. The library also has a search box (names, tags and places on the body),
  **Favourites** (star a card) and **Recent** (the last eight stamped). The chip you last used and
  your stars are remembered per person.
- **Stamp once or keep stamping.** A switch in the library sets it, and it is remembered per
  person. *Stamp once* (the default) puts the mark down after one stamp and selects the new layer.
  **Shift + click** always stamps and keeps the mark picked. A click on a layer now selects it, even
  with a mark picked, and **Alt + click** stamps over a layer on purpose. A line under the viewport
  says what a click does now.
- **The layer's Look:** only the mark's own settings are shown: size (up to the mark's own
  ceiling), turn, opacity, Mirror, Age when the mark has stages, presets, its controls and one
  swatch per ink. Every channel setting is still there, under **Advanced**.
- **The live preview matches the bake.** The Projection view now runs the bake's own compositor
  over copies of the skin's textures (at up to 2048 px) and shows them in the skin's own
  materials. Ink, Tone, stages, inks and controls are all visible as you move a slider, without
  baking. Virtual-textured MetaHuman maps work too.
- **Mirror** per layer, for the other side of the body. A mark that contains letters
  (`mirror_text: false`) never flips.
- **Apply reaches a MetaHuman placed in a level.** Its face materials are dynamic instances that
  its construction script makes. Apply now sets the version's baked textures on those instances,
  and Revert puts their own textures back. This lasts only until the construction script runs
  again (moving the actor, compiling its Blueprint or reloading the level), and it is never saved.
  The result says so. To keep a version on a character, apply it to its Blueprint or its assets.

#### Changed
- **The hardcoded Age effect is gone.** Age now moves a layer through its mark's stages, and a mark
  that should fade or spread does so through its own controls. A mark with one stage ignores Age.
  This also ends the pale box that Age drew over the whole stamp area of a Tone mark.
- Controls' tone changes are always masked, so no setting can tint the projector square.

#### Fixed
- **Text came out mirrored** on some wrapped stamps, such as on the left temple. The wrap built
  its frame from a left-handed basis, and the quaternion made from that basis flipped an axis on
  some orientations. The frame is now a true rotation, and a guard turns any mirrored patch back.
- **Face stamps no longer reach the teeth, eyes or lashes.** A layer reaches only the skin its
  centre is on, plus the surfaces joined to that skin at a seam, such as a MetaHuman's face and
  body at the neck. This works at every LOD.

#### Compatibility
- Decals and sets from 0.2 load as they are. A decal is a mark with one stage, and a v3 ink decal
  has one ink, "Ink", which is the layer's Tint. The starter manifest takes `"version": 4` and
  still reads versions 3 and 2.
- Layers that used Age on a one-stage decal now bake without the old ageing.

#### Known issues
- A per-actor apply does not survive the construction script. A component that re-applies after
  construction would make it last; it is not built yet.
- The preview builds its texture copies through the engine's texture build, so each slider stop
  adds a small entry to the local derived-data cache.

## 0.2.x

### 0.2.0

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Blend modes for colour, per decal, with a per-layer override.** *Over* is as before.
  *Ink* multiplies the ink into the skin, so its tone and pores carry on through a tattoo on
  any skin colour. The ink is softened and slightly desaturated by its *Ink Depth* (mm under
  the skin). *Tone* shifts the skin's own colour (lighter, redder, paler, less saturated)
  instead of painting one. A scar made once reads right on fair and on dark skin. A Tone decal
  can carry a `_Tone` image of the shift, or take it from its painted colour against the skin
  it was painted on.
- **Keep Skin Detail** for Over: puts the skin's pore-scale light and dark back over paint.
- **Relative channels:** each one-value channel (roughness, specular, metallic, AO, fuzz,
  scatter) can be *Absolute* (as before), an *Offset* of the skin's value or a *Scale* of it.
  So a scar is shinier than the skin around it without flattening that skin's own variation.
- **Fuzz and Scatter channels.** New MetaHuman sets write fuzz into SRMF alpha and scatter into
  `Scatter Baked`, so scar tissue and metal can lose their peach fuzz and scatter less.
- **Pore Smoothing:** takes the skin's own pores out of the normal map under a scar and keeps
  the larger forms.
- **Age and Seed per layer.** Age spreads, fades and blue-greens ink, and turns red scars pale.
  Seed warps a stamp slightly, so two copies of one decal differ.
- The starter manifest (`starter_decals.json`) takes the new keys: `blend`, `ink_depth_mm`,
  `keep_detail`, `tone_saturation`, `tone_reference`, `pore_smooth`, `&lt;channel>_offset`,
  `&lt;channel>_scale`, `&lt;channel>_mode`, `fuzz` and `scatter`. Images can be named `_Tone`,
  `_Fuzz` and `_Scatter`.
- Offscreen captures take a light direction and brightness, for grazing-light checks of relief
  and scar shine.
- **Starter decal library v3: 42 decals, up from 22, all generated by us.** Scars come in healing
  stages (cut fresh, scabbed, healing, old; surgical fresh, healing, old) plus claw, burn,
  keloid, sunken, acne and bullet scars. Bruises come in three stages by day. Tattoos come
  fresh, healed, faded and blown out, with five new designs. Ports and plates have a groove,
  lip and seam ring in the skin around them. Scars and bruises use Tone, tattoos use Ink, and
  metal uses Over. Every existing decal name is kept.

#### Compatibility
- Decals and sets made before this version bake exactly as before. New fields default to
  Over and Absolute, with no fuzz or scatter. Existing sets keep the target maps they were saved
  with; reset a set's Target Maps to get fuzz and scatter.

#### Known issues
- The live Projection preview still shows every decal as Over. Ink, Tone, the relative
  channels, Age and Seed show only in the bake.

## 0.1.x

### 0.1.0

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- First version. Stamp scars, tattoos, ports and other marks onto any mesh as layers, preview
  them live, then bake them into new copies of the mesh's textures and new material instances.
  The original textures and materials are never edited.
- Versions: every bake is a named version in its own folder, with the layers it was made from,
