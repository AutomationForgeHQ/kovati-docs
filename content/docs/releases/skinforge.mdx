## 0.1.x

### 0.1.0

*1 January 1970*

#### Added
- First version. Stamp scars, tattoos, ports and other marks onto any mesh as layers, preview
  them live, then bake them into new copies of the mesh's textures and new material instances.
  The original textures and materials are never edited.
- Versions: every bake is a named version in its own folder, with the layers it was made from,
  so a character can keep *Clean*, *Scarred* and *Veteran* side by side and bring any of them
  back to edit.
- Apply and Revert: point the assets and actors that use the original materials at a version's
  materials, and back. A target's state is read from its references, so it stays right after
  undo or a manual edit.
- A larger starter decal set: fresh and old scars, tattoos, and mechanical ports, plates and
  vents, generated by `Resources/StarterDecals/make_starter_decals.py`.
