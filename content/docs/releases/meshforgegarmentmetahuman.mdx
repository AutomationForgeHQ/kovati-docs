## 0.1.x

### 0.1.0

*1 January 1970* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- **Parametric outfit**, a MeshForge post step after wardrobe skinning. One wrapped garment becomes one skeletal mesh with a morph per MetaHuman body slider, plus a resizable outfit asset, a wardrobe item and a fit record. It fits bodies made with the MetaHuman sliders within about 1.3 cm of Epic's own fit, measured on bodies it was not made from.
- Moving a face rig onto a changed body without the cloud, so a resized MetaHuman keeps its own face.

#### Known issues
- Experimental. Not tested in a packaged build.
- MetaHumans that were not made with the body sliders (rigged or scanned people) miss by 2.5–3.8 cm and need a per-person correction. On a plain Blueprint the outfit only fits through the Narrative wardrobe route; outside it the morph weights stay at zero.
- A garment cut for one body type keeps that cut (a women's suit stays a women's suit on a masculine body).
