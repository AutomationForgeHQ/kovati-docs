## 0.1.x

### 0.1.2

*11 September 2026* · Installed through [the account app](https://app.kovati.dev)

#### Added
- A localised bank now knows what each of its lines is a translation of, and records it beside the
  line: the method it will use, what the source was, whether this bank dubbed it, and which
  recording the dub followed. It is visible on the asset, so "why is this line a dub" is answered
  where you are already looking.
- **Generate All** on a localised bank generates the lines whose source is synthesis and **dubs**
  the lines whose source is a recorded performance, in one costed confirmation — instead of paying
  a speech provider to read every line out again and discarding the acting. Afterwards it points
  every face bank serving that bank at the new audio, so the separate Create/Update Face Bank step
  is gone.

#### Fixed
- A dub of a dry voice recording played two languages at once. The dubbing service separates
  "background" from speech and lays it back under the dub; on a dry recording that "background" is
  a smeared copy of the original performance. It is now always dropped.
- A dub's source language is stated rather than detected — see SpeechForge's Authoring Language
  Code.
- The service's own transcript of the finished dub is fetched, logged, and kept on the line as its
  alignment text, so nobody has to listen to a take to find out whether the dub understood its
  source. A transcript that cannot be read degrades to a warning and never to a failed dub: the
  audio is already on disk.

#### Compatibility
- Needs a SpeechForge with the Authoring Language Code setting and the two bank seams.
